## 🚀 How to Get Your Site on Google

### Step 1: Submit to Google Search Console (Already Done ✅)
Your site is already verified. To check status:
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Login with your Google account
3. Add property: `https://mbita-deogratias.vercel.app`
4. Submit sitemap: `https://mbita-deogratias.vercel.app/sitemap.xml`

### Step 2: Submit Sitemap Manually
1. Go to Search Console
2. Click "Sitemaps" in left sidebar
3. Enter: `sitemap.xml`
4. Click "Submit"

### Step 3: Request Indexing for Important Pages
1. In Search Console, click "URL Inspection" (top)
2. Enter: `https://mbita-deogratias.vercel.app`
3. Click "Request Indexing"
4. Repeat for key pages:
   - `/about`
   - `/research`
   - `/publications`
   - `/contact`

### Step 4: Wait for Google Crawl
- **First indexing**: 1-7 days
- **Full site indexing**: 2-4 weeks
- **Updates**: 1-3 days after changes

## 📈 Monitor Your SEO Performance

### Google Search Console Metrics
Check weekly:
- **Impressions**: How many times your site appeared in search
- **Clicks**: How many people clicked to your site
- **Position**: Average ranking in search results
- **Coverage**: Pages indexed vs. total pages

### Key Performance Indicators
- **Target**: 100+ impressions/day within 3 months
- **Good CTR**: 3-5% (clicks ÷ impressions)
- **Target Ranking**: Top 10 for your name + keywords

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "My site doesn't show up in Google"
- **Wait**: First indexing takes 1-7 days
- **Check**: Search for exact URL in Google
- **Verify**: robots.txt is not blocking (it's not)
- **Action**: Request indexing in Search Console

### "Only homepage is indexed"
- **Wait**: Full site indexing takes 2-4 weeks
- **Check**: Sitemap is submitted correctly
- **Action**: Request indexing for specific pages

### "Rankings are low"
- **Normal**: New sites start with low rankings
- **Solution**: Add quality content regularly
- **Timeline**: Expect improvement in 3-6 months
